Teaching AI our business rules

Unit tests on every commit

I set up Claude and Copilot to generate unit tests for commits to feature branches. They catch code-level edge cases; I still check that the logic matches the business rule.

ms project · pytorch

Question answering on SQuAD 2.0

I built a BiDAF reading-comprehension model in PyTorch, then swapped its BiLSTM encoder for a GRU. The GRU trained faster and edged out the baseline on Exact Match and F1; lowering the learning rate or raising dropout made it worse.
